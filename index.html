Ali Abdari

I am Ali Abdari. I have studied computer science at Kharazmi University. Image processing and machine learning are two of my favorite areas. I've done a lot of image processing projects in recent years. During my master's degree, I focused my research on action recognition in video. My thesis has been devoted to using compressed domain elements for action recognition in compressed videos.
Now, I am a Ph.D. student at University of Naples Federico II, and I am passing my Ph.D in AILAB at the University of Udine. I am working mainly on 3D complex scenarios retrieval.
